SIDE A

Getting Started

A1What Runback does

Save 2–10 favorite songs, in order, as a set, and play it with one tap (works with songs on Apple Music). Pick songs from search, charts, and your library, or save straight from Share in the Music app. After the set, ∞ (Autoplay) keeps going with similar songs.

There are also smart mixes that pick songs by rules, a Home Screen widget, Shortcuts (Siri), and export to a Music playlist.

A2Sending a set to a friend

On a set, tap “…” → Share Set to send it by Messages, AirDrop, and more. When your friend opens it in Runback, they can review it and add it as their own set. The song list is included, so people without Runback can see what’s in it. Photo covers and play history aren’t sent.

A3Playing several sets in a row

From the Moods view menu, choose Play Sets in a Row and tap sets in the order you want. To add a set without replacing the current queue, use “…” → Play Next or Play Last on the set.

A4What is “Keep the run from …?”

Using what you played recently, Runback finds where the genres or artists changed sharply and similar songs kept going, and suggests turning that run into a set. It reads your history when you open the app and during background refresh when iOS allows, and keeps the result only on this iPhone. Turn it off in Settings → History & Privacy.

A5Playing from Control Center

Add “Play Recent Set” to Control Center or the Lock Screen (iOS 18 or later). It opens Runback and plays the set you used most recently.

A6Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Playing songs requires an Apple Music plan that can play catalog songs. Without one, you can open the app, but sets can’t be played.

A7What happens to my current queue?

Playing a set replaces the Music queue. If something is playing or paused, Runback asks first.

For 8 seconds afterwards, Undo plays the song you were on again, from the same position. iOS doesn’t let apps read the full queue, so only that one song and position can be restored.

A8Music doesn’t continue after the set (∞)

Replacing the queue may turn off ∞ (Autoplay) in Music. Turn it on in Music’s Playing Next view. Apps can’t read whether ∞ is on, so Runback doesn’t show it.

A9Saving from Share in Music

In Music or Safari, choose Share → Runback on a song (or album), pick a set, and it’s added right away. For an album, pick the song first. If you choose “New Set with This Song,” the editor opens the next time you open Runback.

If Runback isn’t in the list, scroll to “More” at the end of the share sheet and turn it on.

A10What are starting points and conditions in smart mixes?

Songs are gathered from starting points — genre charts, artists’ top songs (and similar artists), and search words — then narrowed by optional conditions such as release year or length. The catalog can’t be queried by conditions alone, so this is how it works.

Library mixes can also use conditions like date added and play count.

SIDE B

Troubleshooting

B1It says some songs “can’t be played right now”

Those songs were removed from Apple Music or aren’t available in your region. Tap Fix on the set, then “Find a Replacement” from the song’s menu to search by title and artist and swap it in. Runback never removes or replaces songs on its own.

It doesn’t show this when you’re simply offline.

B2I deleted a set by mistake

Tap Undo at the bottom within 8 seconds. After that, restore it from a backup.

B3New iPhone / syncing with other iPhones

Sets sync automatically through iCloud to other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. On a new iPhone, install Runback and sign in with the same Apple Account and your sets come back. Check the status in Settings → Backup & Restore.

To keep a file copy, use Export All Sets on the same screen. Restoring only adds sets; it never overwrites ones already there. Smart mix rules are exported separately from Library → Smart Mixes → “…”.

B4The widget doesn’t update

The widget updates when you open the app or save a set. Open Runback once, then go back to the Home Screen.

B5“Your usual at this hour” and history

Suggestions by time of day use when you play sets in the app and Music’s Recently Played. The records stay on this iPhone. Turn this off or clear it in Settings → History & Privacy.

B6Allowing Apple Music access again

Open the Settings app → Apps → Runback and turn on Media & Apple Music.

B7If playback isn’t working

Go to Settings → Help & Support → Playback Diagnostics, tap Copy Log, and paste it into the form below. It contains only the playback state, not your listening history.
